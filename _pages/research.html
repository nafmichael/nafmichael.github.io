I work on large multimodal models and the agents built on them. My research has three threads: learning representations that put images and text in one space, the infrastructure that trains those models at scale, and agents whose work can be checked against a ground truth, which is what I now build for chip design as a founder. Before that, I worked on computer vision: GAN-based domain adaptation and scene understanding.
